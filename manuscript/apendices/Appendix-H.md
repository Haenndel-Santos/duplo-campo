$$ r = \frac{b}{a} \neq 1. $$

$$ V(\mathcal{K}) = \sum_{n=0}^4 \beta_n e_n(\mathcal{K}), \quad \mathcal{K}=\sqrt{g^{-1}f}. $$

$$ V \rightarrow F(\chi)V. $$

$$ \ddot\chi + 3H\dot\chi + U\'(\chi) = m^2M_{eff}^2 F\'(\chi)V. $$

$$ \dot\eta = \Gamma \dot\chi^2, \quad \Gamma\>0. $$
