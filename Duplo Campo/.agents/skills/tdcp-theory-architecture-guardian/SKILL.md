# TDCP Theory Architecture Guardian

## When to Use This Skill

Use this skill when adding, revising, or reviewing TDCP material that could affect the global structure of the theory. Apply it before accepting new conceptual definitions, chapter architecture, background assumptions, or claims about how the model connects to observables.

## What the Skill Must Check

- Preserve the two correlated primordial regimes as the basic structural premise.
- Treat bifurcation as a structural transition, not a vague metaphor.
- Keep the dynamic vacuum tied to structural separation, tension, or relational differentiation.
- Treat time as relational or separation-based when the text uses TDCP foundational language.
- Preserve the role of two metrics, g and f, when entering the bimetric formulation.
- Preserve the primordial scalar phi as the field that modulates or organizes the transition.
- Require a credible GR recovery limit.
- Require testability through cosmological observables rather than purely interpretive claims.
- Track whether new material belongs to ontology, formalism, background cosmology, perturbations, or observables.

## What the Skill Must Not Do

- Do not invent new ontological principles to make a passage sound deeper.
- Do not weaken the mathematical formalism to preserve a conceptual claim.
- Do not promote speculation into a result.
- Do not accept claims of empirical support without a defined observable, likelihood, or comparison target.
- Do not silently merge the two regimes into a single-field or single-metric picture.

## Required Output Format

Return:

1. **Architecture Verdict:** Preserve / Needs revision / Incompatible.
2. **Core Checks:** Short bullets for each TDCP principle touched.
3. **Conflicts:** Quote or identify the problematic claim, then explain the conflict.
4. **Required Fixes:** Minimal edits or constraints needed.
5. **Residual Risk:** Any open conceptual or mathematical dependency.

## Common Failure Modes

- Bifurcation is used as poetic language without a structural role.
- The second metric appears and disappears without a limit or branch condition.
- GR recovery is asserted but not connected to a limit.
- Observability is claimed without mu(k,a), Sigma(k,a), eta_slip, f sigma_8, BAO, RSD, weak lensing, or related targets.
- The text jumps from ontology directly to prediction without a formal bridge.

## Checklist

- Two regimes preserved?
- Bifurcation defined as structural transition?
- Dynamic vacuum tied to separation/tension?
- Time treated consistently?
- g, f, and phi roles stable?
- GR limit stated or recoverable?
- Testable observables identified?
- Layer of the theory clearly located?
