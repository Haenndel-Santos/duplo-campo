13.6 Subtlety of the dynamical branch

Fixing gauge $N_{g} = 1$:

$$H_{g} = \frac{\dot{a}}{a},H_{f} = \frac{1}{\xi}\frac{\dot{b}}{b}.

From the branch condition:

$$H_{g} = \xi H_{f} = \frac{\dot{b}}{b}.

Thus:

$$\frac{\dot{b}}{b} = \frac{\dot{a}}{a} \Rightarrow \frac{d}{dt}\ln\left( \frac{b}{a} \right) = 0 \Rightarrow \dot{r} = 0.
$$

Important result:

In strictly diagonal FLRW with pure HR coupling, the "dynamical" branch forces $r =$ constant.

$$\frac{dU}{dr} = 3\beta_{1} + 6\beta_{2}r.$$

The effective mass of the relative mode takes the typical form:

$$\boxed{m_{S}^{2} \sim m^{2}F(\phi)\text{ }\left( \beta_{1}+2\beta_{2}r \right)}$$

(normalization factors depending on $M_{g},M_{f},r$ are omitted, as they are positive.)
